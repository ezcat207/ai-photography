# Chapter 5: Focal Length — How Far, How Squished

**Map position**: Lens · how strong the telescope is.

Why does their moon look like a dinner plate while yours is a grain of rice? Not the phone — focal length. And why does your selfie face always look a little… off? Also focal length.

![Telephoto compression: runners squeezed into a queue](images/ch05-focal-length.webp)

Look at this shot: morning runners on a track, squished by a long lens into an overlapping queue, the school building dragged right up behind them. That's telephoto magic — space compressed like a pressed sandwich.

## What focal length is

**One sentence**: how wide or narrow the lens sees — it decides how "squished" near and far look together.

**In plain English** — three gears:

- **Wide angle (small number, like 16mm)**: huge view, near things look giant. A selfie stick IS a wide angle — long legs, whole room in one shot. Landscapes, buildings, big scenes.
- **Standard (~50mm)**: closest to human eyes, most natural. Everyday life, friends hanging out.
- **Telephoto (big number, like 200mm)**: drags far things close AND squishes space. Moon shots, sports, sneaky cat photos. The portrait sweet spot is 85mm — face undistorted, background creamy.

**Three cold facts**:
1. The three cameras on your phone's back ARE three focal lengths: ultra-wide, wide, telephoto. Switching lenses = switching focal lengths.
2. Wide angle + face up close = big nose. Want to prank a friend? Ultra-wide, right in the face. (Don't actually.)
3. Telephoto magnifies shake: at 200mm, one tremble and the frame turns to ocean — brace against a wall.

### Selfie lab

Your front camera is a wide angle — that's the actual culprit behind "doesn't look like me." Fix: back camera + 3-second timer, two steps back. Or embrace the wide and extend the selfie stick to max.

## Translating to prompts

- Big wide scene: "ultra wide angle, grand scene, long legs, sense of space"
- Telephoto squash: "telephoto compression, distant things pulled close, giant moon"
- Natural portrait: "85mm portrait, undistorted face, creamy background"

## Experiment you can redo

Same school building, three shots: ultra-wide / 1x / telephoto. Watch the building get "squished" — your closest encounter with compression.

## Blooper warnings

- **Faceplant blooper**: ultra-wide selfie up close = nose tripled. Extend the stick or switch to 1x.
- **Shake blooper**: handheld telephoto doubles your blur rate. Brace first, shoot second.
