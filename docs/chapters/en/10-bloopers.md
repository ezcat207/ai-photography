# Chapter 10: Blooper Clinic — 12 Problems, Fixed in One Place

Chapters 1–9 were "how to drive." This is the repair shop. Twelve real face-plants, each fixed in five steps: symptom → cause → self-check → fix → prevention. [CAMERA] = shot-on-phone fails, [AI] = AI-generation fails.

![Six-panel grid of AI bloopers](images/ch10-bloopers.webp)

Before wrenching, remember the Chapter 1 map: #1–6 are physics fails (something wrong with light, lens, or you), #7–12 are "didn't say the decision clearly" fails (the AI improvised). Every cause has a home on the map.

## 1. Everything's blurry [CAMERA]

- **Symptom**: zoom in, it's all mush.
- **Cause**: shutter too slow + shaky hands.
- **Self-check**: look at the shutter speed — under 1/60, that's it.
- **Fix**: raise to 1/125+, or rest the phone on a table / lean on a wall.
- **Prevention**: check shutter speed before the frame, not after.

## 2. Face goes black in backlight [CAMERA]

- **Symptom**: gorgeous background, person-shaped shadow.
- **Cause**: the camera metered for the bright part and gave up on the face.
- **Self-check**: tap the face on screen — does the brightness jump?
- **Fix**: tap-to-meter on the face; or have a friend bounce phone light from the front; or commit to the silhouette (honestly beautiful).
- **Prevention**: in backlight, decide first: the face, or the silhouette.

## 3. Back row of the group photo is ghosts [CAMERA]

- **Symptom**: front row sharp, back row haunted.
- **Cause**: aperture too wide, depth of field too shallow.
- **Self-check**: is the aperture around f/2?
- **Fix**: stop down to f/8 — more people, bigger number.
- **Prevention**: burn it in: more people = bigger f-number.

## 4. Night shot is all static [CAMERA]

- **Symptom**: shadows full of snow.
- **Cause**: ISO cranked to the moon.
- **Self-check**: ISO reading 6400+?
- **Fix**: rerun the playbook — find light > stabilize > raise ISO (Chapter 4).
- **Prevention**: at night, find a light source before touching ISO.

## 5. The horizon is drunk [CAMERA]

- **Symptom**: the sea is diagonal, the tower leans.
- **Cause**: nobody watched the horizon.
- **Self-check**: grid lines on?
- **Fix**: rotate straight in editing, one minute.
- **Prevention**: one glance at the horizon before shooting. Three seconds.

## 6. The background is photobombing [CAMERA]

- **Symptom**: great subject, lamppost "growing" out of their head.
- **Cause**: never looked at the background.
- **Self-check**: anything weird behind the subject's head?
- **Fix**: open the aperture to melt it, shift one step for a new angle, or walk closer.
- **Prevention**: background first, subject second — reverse the order.

## 7. Six fingers [AI]

- **Symptom**: hand close-up, wrong finger count.
- **Cause**: models never really learned hands.
- **Self-check**: any hand close-up — count the fingers.
- **Fix**: regenerate; or hide the hands (pockets, behind back); or inpaint just the hand.
- **Prevention**: add "natural hands" to the prompt, or avoid hand close-ups entirely.

## 8. Gibberish on the sign [AI]

- **Symptom**: sign text reads like alien language.
- **Cause**: AI can't read. Stop asking it to.
- **Self-check**: any text in frame — zoom in and read it.
- **Fix**: use short English words in the prompt; or add real text yourself in post.
- **Prevention**: important text never comes from the AI — leave blank space and add it.

## 9. Said "dusk," got noon [AI]

- **Symptom**: the light is completely wrong.
- **Cause**: no lighting words in the prompt, AI went default.
- **Self-check**: does the prompt contain ANY lighting word?
- **Fix**: add "golden hour, backlight" or "harsh noon sun" — one phrase does it.
- **Prevention**: always write one lighting phrase. Skip it and you're rolling dice.

## 10. Wanted anime, got a photo [AI]

- **Symptom**: style went rogue.
- **Cause**: no style words.
- **Self-check**: is the FIRST phrase of the prompt the style?
- **Fix**: lead with style: "anime style, …"
- **Prevention**: style is sentence one, not the footnote.

## 11. Long prompt got ignored [AI]

- **Symptom**: wrote a paragraph, AI heard half of it.
- **Cause**: weight dilution — the important stuff drowned.
- **Self-check**: prompt longer than 5 lines?
- **Fix**: compress to 3 lines max, important stuff first.
- **Prevention**: less is more. Cut until it can't be cut.

## 12. Same character, different face every time [AI]

- **Symptom**: series of images, face changes each time.
- **Cause**: the description changes every time.
- **Self-check**: is the appearance description word-for-word identical across prompts?
- **Fix**: build a "character card" (one fixed appearance paragraph), copy-paste every time.
- **Prevention**: same character, same paragraph, every time.
