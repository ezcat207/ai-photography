# Chapter 4: ISO & Night — What to Do When the Sun Clocks Out

**Map position**: Light · how much. ISO isn't night vision — it's a signal amplifier.

You hold up your phone at a concert to get your idol's face. No flash allowed. What you get is static. Your phone isn't broken — the megaphone is just cranked too high.

![Concert crowd, light beams and silhouettes](images/ch04-iso-night.webp)

Look at this shot: light beams cutting the dark, phones glowing, the crowd in silhouette. Zoom into the shadows and you'll see fine grain — that's high-ISO noise. The photographer's call: a grainy real moment beats a clean black nothing.

## What ISO is

**One sentence**: the camera's "night hearing." The price is noise.

**In plain English**:

- **ISO 100–400 (daytime)**: clean as a whistle. Leave it here when the sun's out.
- **ISO 800–3200 (dusk/indoors)**: visible now, grain creeping into the shadows. Acceptable.
- **ISO 6400+ (dead of night)**: static party. Get the shot first, pretty later.

**The night playbook** — in this order, don't just crank ISO first:

1. **Find light**: street lamps, store signs, stage beams — move into light. Cheapest trick there is.
2. **Stabilize**: rest the phone on a railing or table and use a slow shutter (Chapter 3's trick) instead of high ISO.
3. **Add light**: truly nothing? Have a friend shine their flashlight from the side — instant three-stop upgrade.

Memorize the order: **find light > stabilize > raise ISO**. Cranking ISO first is the lazy move, and noise is the interest.

### Selfie lab

Concert selfie with your idol in frame — cranked ISO gets it, but grain turns your face to Minecraft. Stand inside a stage beam and the ISO doesn't have to work as hard. The megaphone is the last resort, not the first.

## Experiment you can redo

Tonight, find a dark corner. Two shots of the same scene: ISO auto (phone cranks it sky-high) vs. manual ISO 800 with the phone braced steady. Compare the grain — you'll see with your own eyes that "stabilize" beats "crank."

## Blooper warnings

- **Disaster combo**: max ISO + max digital zoom = Minecraft. Zoom with your feet — walk closer, don't pinch.
- **Forgot to reset**: last night's ISO 6400 still on this morning = blown-out daylight. Habit: sun up, ISO back to 100.
