# Appendix B: Prompt Glossary

English prompt words, organized by the 8 decisions — each with a one-line plain-English meaning.

## Aperture

| Say this | It means |
|----------|----------|
| blurred background | background out of focus |
| shallow depth of field | only the subject is sharp |
| bokeh lights | background melted into glowing orbs |
| deep depth of field | sharp front to back |

## Shutter

| Say this | It means |
|----------|----------|
| frozen mid-air | motion completely stopped |
| long exposure | shutter open a long time |
| light trails | traffic smeared into ribbons of light |
| motion blur | blurry on purpose, sense of speed |

## ISO / Night

| Say this | It means |
|----------|----------|
| night scene | shot after dark |
| subtle film grain | a little noise, feels real |
| clean and clear | no noise, polished |
| concert atmosphere | stage lights, crowd energy |

## Focal length

| Say this | It means |
|----------|----------|
| ultra wide angle | huge view, dramatic near-far |
| wide angle | broad scene |
| telephoto compression | distant things squished together |
| 85mm portrait | natural face, creamy background |

## Angle

| Say this | It means |
|----------|----------|
| low angle looking up | subject looks tall and powerful |
| high angle looking down | smaller face, cute |
| eye level | honest and natural |
| dutch angle | tilted frame, edgy |

## Composition

| Say this | It means |
|----------|----------|
| rule of thirds | subject on a grid intersection |
| leading lines | lines pulling eyes to the subject |
| frame within a frame | doorway/window as a picture frame |
| negative space | empty area for breathing room |
| symmetrical composition | mirrored, orderly |

## Light

| Say this | It means |
|----------|----------|
| front light | lit from the front, flat |
| side light | half lit, sculptural |
| backlight | light from behind the subject |
| rim light | glowing outline |
| golden hour | warm sunset/sunrise light |
| blue hour | deep blue just after sunset |
| soft light | diffused, flattering |

## Color / Style

| Say this | It means |
|----------|----------|
| warm nostalgic tones | yellow-orange, cozy memories |
| cold blue mood | lonely, cinematic night |
| high saturation | vivid, loud colors |
| muted tones | quiet, grayish colors |
| black and white | no color, high contrast |
| cinematic | looks like a movie still |
| photorealistic | looks like a real photo |
| anime style | Japanese animation look |
