# Chapter 11: Final Project — Your Signature Piece

All 8 decisions learned, all 12 bloopers fixed. Now build one piece that represents you.

![Teen holding a photo in front of a cozy photo wall](../images/ch11-final.webp)

## The mission: one signature piece

Pick a theme **you actually care about** (your cat, skateboarding, bubble tea, night streets, your best friend…), make all 8 decisions one by one, shoot it (or generate it), and attach your "director's notes."

## Director's notes template (fill in the blanks)

My theme: ____ (e.g., my cat's afternoon)

1. **Aperture**: I chose ____ (wide/narrow), because ____
2. **Shutter**: I chose ____ (fast/slow), because ____
3. **ISO**: the light was ____, so I ____ (found light / stabilized / raised ISO), because ____
4. **Focal length**: I used ____ (wide/telephoto), because ____
5. **Angle**: I used ____ (looking up / looking down / eye level), because ____
6. **Composition**: I used ____ (rule of thirds / leading lines / framing / negative space), because ____
7. **Light**: I used ____ (front / side / back), at ____ (time of day), because ____
8. **Color**: I set ____ (warm/cold), because ____

**Example** (excerpt): My theme is "my cat's afternoon." Aperture: wide open, because I wanted her to pop out of my messy couch. Shutter: fast, 1/1000, because she's about to pounce next second. Light: side light from the 3pm window, because her fur gets sculptural…

## Turn-in checklist

- ① The photo (your signature piece)
- ② Filled director's notes (all 8 decisions)
- ③ One sentence: what is this photo trying to say

Before turning in, self-check against the Chapter 1 map: light (how much / from where / what color) + lens (how wide / how long / how strong) + you (where / how arranged) — all 8 blanks filled?

## Peer review: the 3-comment method

Looking at someone else's piece, leave three comments:

1. **One good**: which decision was nailed (e.g., "the backlight is perfect, the hair is literally glowing")
2. **One question**: one thing you didn't get (e.g., "why cold tones here?")
3. **One suggestion**: "if it were me, I'd try ____"

## What's next

Turn this in and you've officially climbed from Bronze to Silver. Next stop is Gold: pick ONE genre (portraits? street? food? sneakers?) and shoot 100 — the narrower the lane, the faster you level.

This book's 8 decisions are the map. Your 100 shots are the road. Go.
