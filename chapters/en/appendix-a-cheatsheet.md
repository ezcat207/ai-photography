# Appendix A: Prompt Cheat Sheet (print this page)

## The universal sentence

**[subject]**, **[action]**, in **[setting]**, **[lighting]**, **[camera angle]**, **[composition]**, **[color/style]**.

中文句式：拍 **[主体]**，**[动作]**，在 **[场景]**，**[光线]**，**[角度]**，**[构图]**，**[色彩/风格]**。

## Fill-in examples

**Example 1 (portrait)**:
"My friend, running toward me laughing, on the track field, golden-hour backlight, low angle looking up, rule of thirds on the right, warm nostalgic tones."

**Example 2 (food)**:
"A bowl of beef noodles, steam rising, at a street stall, window side light, close-up, shallow depth of field with blurred background, warm tones."

**Example 3 (night)**:
"City night, traffic streaming, from a footbridge, blue hour, high angle looking down, leading lines, cold blue lonely mood."

## How to use it

1. Fill it in Chinese first if that's easier — then write it in English (AIs listen better in English).
2. You don't need all 8 blanks every time. Start with 4: subject, light, angle, color.
3. Style goes FIRST: "anime style, …"
