# Chapter 9: Color — Warm or Cold

**Map position**: Light · what color.

Same convenience store — how do you shoot "lonely 2am emo" and "hopeful morning" from it? The answer isn't on the shelves. It's in the temperature. The filters you use every day? They're just shortcuts for this decision.

![Late-night convenience store in cold blue tones](../images/ch09-color.webp)

Look at this shot: a convenience store at night, cold blue light, a kid alone at the oden pot. The oden is hot, but the whole photo feels cold — color sets the emotion first, content follows.

## What color is

**One sentence**: the "temperature" of the frame — it sets the emotion before the story.

**In plain English**:

- **Warm (yellow/orange/red)**: lively, nostalgic, tasty. Hot pot, sunsets, old streets — warmer is righter.
- **Cold (blue/teal)**: calm, lonely, techy. Late nights, rain, emo hours — colder is truer.
- **White balance**: the camera's "thermometer." When it guesses wrong, whites go yellow or blue — sometimes wrong-on-purpose becomes a style.
- **Complementary colors**: orange and blue are born rivals (sunset against blue sky) — put them together and they hype each other. Movie posters live on this.

**Two cold facts**:
1. Decide the emotion first, then the temperature — never the reverse.
2. Saturation is "volume": maxed out is shouting, low is whispering. Don't max it on arrival or everything goes radioactive.

### Selfie lab

Warm filter = golden-hour extrovert, cold filter = mysterious cool kid. Decide who you are today, then pick the filter. Warm before a date, cold for emo nights — color is the emotion remote.

## Translating to prompts

- Warm: "warm nostalgic tones, golden-hour vibe"
- Cold: "cold blue, lonely mood, late night"
- Punchy: "high saturation, vivid"
- Muted: "low saturation, muted gray tones"
- B&W: "black and white, high contrast"

## Experiment you can redo

Same store, phone editor: drag temperature all the way cold, save; all the way warm, save. Ask a friend: which one is "2am emo," which is "weekend date"? Color class, graduated in 5 minutes.

## Blooper warnings

- **Fighting blooper**: auto white balance under mixed lighting goes split-brain — half yellow, half blue. Lock white balance manually or unify in post.
- **Radioactive blooper**: saturation +100, grass glows like a highlighter. Saturation is seasoning, not the meal.
