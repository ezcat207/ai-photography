# Appendix C: Tool Comparison

> ⚠️ Draft: AI image tools change fast. Specific names, prices, and links get verified right before publishing. This appendix only gives you the selection framework.

## Three kinds of tools

**1. Your phone (you already own it)**
- For: all of Chapters 2–9. Portrait Mode practices aperture, Night Mode practices shutter + ISO, three lenses practice focal length.
- Cost: $0.
- One line: you don't need any AI for the first 9 chapters. Your phone is enough.

**2. Free AI image generators (practice prompt translation)**
- For: turning "prompt translation" into images, testing whether the AI understands you.
- Pick by: free daily quota that covers homework, no VPN needed, works in your language.
- Specific list: pending verification (final check before publishing).

**3. Advanced tools (for the final project)**
- For: inpainting (fixing hands), upscaling, more control.
- When: Chapter 10's blooper clinic, Chapter 11's final project.
- Specific list: pending verification (final check before publishing).

## Three questions for picking a tool

1. Can it generate at least 5 free images a day? (that's the homework load)
2. Does it understand prompts in your language? (this book's sentences)
3. Is it stable? (works today, gone tomorrow doesn't count)

All three pass, then it earns a spot in the final version.
